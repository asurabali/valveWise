# Five-minute viva demo

1. From the repo root run `node frontend/server/server.js`, open `http://localhost:3000` and choose `sample.inp`. The page runs the live C engine over every single-pipe failure. In **Optimization**, greedy places `P5` and `P7` for a 16.5% improvement and matches exhaustive; explain that these are engine outputs.
2. Click pipe `P5` in the graph; it turns red and the status says it is selected. Click **Run simulation**. The impact is 174 L/s. Explain the Isolation valves-to-close and feasibility result (`isolation_feasible`). Greedy now places `P1`, `P4`, `P7` for 26.44%, the same as exhaustive: no single valve helps, so greedy looks ahead over groups of valves.
3. Click **Reset**, then click valve `V1` to toggle stuck, click pipes `P3` and `P7`, and click **Run simulation**. Explain the extra-disruption count and the highlighted node(s).
4. Click the `P6` row in **Critical link ranking** to focus the graph. Click **Score** to reverse sort. Explain the engine-provided score and reason.
5. Compare all four rows in **Optimization**: baseline, optimized disruption, improvement %, runtime, and candidates. Click **Show proposed valves** for the before/after graph. Explain zero as zero, negative as regression, and any displayed exhaustive skip reason.

Stop the server and refresh to show the visible **offline demo** badge and fixture fallback. Upload malformed/partial JSON to show the readable error/empty states.
